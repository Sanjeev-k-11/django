Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Study Streak work?

Your streak increments by 1 for every consecutive day on which you log at least one study session (or complete a Pomodoro focus cycle). If you studied yesterday, your streak remains active today until midnight.

Can other students see my grades or notes?

No. StudyMate enforces strict database-level user isolation (`request.user`). All subjects, notes, test scores, and sessions are accessible exclusively to your authenticated account.

How is my overall GPA calculated?

When you enter exam scores (marks obtained and total marks), StudyMate computes weighted percentages and maps them onto the standard 4.0 academic scale (90%+ = 4.0, 80%+ = 3.5, etc.).

How do I export my data?

Visit the Analytics page and click "Export Tasks CSV" or "Export Study Logs CSV". You can also open the formal Academic Progress Report to save a clean PDF.
